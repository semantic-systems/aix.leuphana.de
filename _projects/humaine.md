HumAIne is an EFRE/STEP research infrastructure initiative focused on human-centered AI. The project is primarily concerned with integrating Artificial Intelligence into everyday working processes, ensuring that these technological advancements lead to improvements in both working and living conditions for individuals.

## Objectives
- Promote the development and adoption of human-centered AI.
- Integrate AI smoothly into existing working processes.
- Ensure AI applications improve overall working and living conditions.
