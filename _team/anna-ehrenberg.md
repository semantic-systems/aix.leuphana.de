## About Me

The path that lead me to my role at AIX has developed through studies of industrial engineering, business and data science and a couple of roles across companies in all sizes. I keep searching for ways to meaningfully integrate diverse perspectives into product development and science to create results that fit our society well. I focus my current research on Geoprivacy by asking the question how a growing amount of Geodata available openly will affect our (location) privacy. 

Besides my research efforts, I coordinate the Minor AI study program at Leuphana University. I like to get involved into the development of study programs and formats that equip students with the future skills needed. My teaching in the interdisciplinary Bachelor's program allows me to experience directly how AI changes their learning experiences. When time allows, I enjoy building small projects in the [Creative Space]([https://www.leuphana.de/en/institutes/iis/news/single-view/2026/10/08/from-plant-app-to-mindful-fork-the-first-minor-ai-conference.html](https://aix.leuphana.de/projects/creative-space/).
