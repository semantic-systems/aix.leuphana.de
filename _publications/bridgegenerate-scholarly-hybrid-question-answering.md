> **Abstract:**
> Answering scholarly hybrid questions requires access to bibliographic facts stored in structured data, such as a Knowledge Graph (KG) and textual information. Existing Scholarly Hybrid Question Answering (SHQA) approaches rely on retrieving KG triples and documents from the Wikipedia text corpus and prompt an LLM (Large Language Model) for answers.
