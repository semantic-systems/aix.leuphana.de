> **Abstract:**
> Uniform Law examines the general foundations of legal unification across fields of private law. It considers why global economic and social connections encourage common legal rules, while identifying obstacles to their adoption. The book surveys the institutions and participants involved, the forms that unified law can take, and the relationship between international instruments and domestic legal systems. It also addresses the interaction with private international law and the treaty framework governing binding conventions. The final part considers non-binding approaches, including model laws, legislative guides, principles and restatements. Prepared for publication after Jürgen Basedow's death, the volume places individual unification projects within a broader conceptual and institutional framework.
