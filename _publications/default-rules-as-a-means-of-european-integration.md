> **Abstract:**
> This contribution examines how rules that parties may vary by agreement can support European integration and the internal market. It considers the functions of existing default rules within EU private law and asks whether their use could be extended. The focus is on the place of such rules in the European legal framework and their potential relevance to closer integration.

**Editorial contribution:** Jannis Gries (recorded as editor in the Leuphana research portal).
