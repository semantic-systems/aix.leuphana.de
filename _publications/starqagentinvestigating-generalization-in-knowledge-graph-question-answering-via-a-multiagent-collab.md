> **Abstract:**
> Generalization to different Knowledge Graphs (KGs) is a core challenge of Knowledge Graph Question Answering (KGQA). Current models struggle to adapt to unseen KGs due to their underlying heterogeneity. Most approaches depend on data-hungry methods, such as supervised fine-tuning and KG-specific few-shot demonstrations.
