> **Abstract:**
> Purpose: The heterogeneity of scholarly information in knowledge graphs (KGs) and unstructured textual sources poses challenges in building robust Scholarly Question Answering (SQA) systems. Existing datasets and models typically address a narrow spectrum, focusing exclusively on KGs or unstructured sources and limiting evaluation to simple factoid questions. This gap leaves current systems unable to answer complex, hybrid scholarly questions that require integrating evidence from multiple heterogeneous data
