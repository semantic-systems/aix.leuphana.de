> **Abstract:**
> Human label variation (HLV) is slowly gaining attention in natural language processing (NLP) research. It challenges the longstanding machine learning (ML) tradition of having a single ground truth via majority voting label aggregation. It influences all components of an ML pipeline, from data to modeling and evaluation. Albeit its relevance, HLV is rarely discussed in the context of computational narrative understanding.
