# Product Context: AIX Leuphana Website

## Purpose
The AIX Leuphana website serves as the digital face of the Artificial Intelligence and Information Systems research group at Leuphana University. It provides a centralized platform to:

- Showcase the research group's academic contributions and achievements
- Facilitate collaboration with other researchers and institutions
- Attract potential students and research partners
- Communicate research findings to both academic and general audiences
- Maintain transparency about ongoing research projects and publications

## Key Features
- **Team Showcase**: Detailed profiles of research group members including their expertise and research focus
- **Publications Database**: Comprehensive listing of academic publications with metadata and access information
- **Research Focus**: Clear presentation of the group's main research areas and methodologies
- **Contact Information**: Easy access to contact details for collaboration and inquiries
- **News and Updates**: Platform for sharing recent developments and achievements

## User Experience Goals
- Professional and academic aesthetic that reflects the institution's standards
- Intuitive navigation that allows visitors to quickly find relevant information
- Responsive design that works across devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- Accessible content that follows web accessibility standards
- Fast loading times and reliable performance

## Content Strategy
- Regular updates to keep publication lists current
- Clear organization of team members by role and research focus
- Highlighting of key achievements and recent publications
- Integration with academic networks and institutional systems
- Support for multilingual content where appropriate
