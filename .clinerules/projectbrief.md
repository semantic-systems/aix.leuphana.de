# Project Brief: AIX Leuphana

## Overview
This is a Jekyll-based website for the AIX (Artificial Intelligence and Information Systems) research group at Leuphana University. The site serves as an informational platform showcasing the team, publications, and research activities.

## Core Requirements
- Maintain a professional academic website
- Showcase team members and their research
- Display publications and research outputs
- Provide information about the research group
- Easy content management through Markdown files

## Goals
- Present research activities in a clear, accessible manner
- Maintain up-to-date information about team members
- Showcase publications and academic achievements
- Provide contact information and research focus areas
- Serve as a central hub for AIX research group information

## Target Audience
- Academic researchers
- Potential collaborators
- Students interested in AI and information systems
- University administration and stakeholders
- General public interested in AI research

## Technical Foundation
- Jekyll static site generator
- Markdown-based content management
- GitHub Pages deployment
- Responsive design for various devices
- Semantic HTML and accessible web practices
