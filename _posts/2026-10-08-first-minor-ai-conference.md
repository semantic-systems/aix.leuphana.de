On 25 September 2026, Leuphana's new **Artificial Intelligence minor** held its first Minor AI Conference. After a welcome by programme director [Ricardo Usbeck](/team/ricardo-usbeck/), students presented their semester projects at around ten stands in image recognition, language models and recommendation systems.

## Not only for computer scientists

Many of the students study non-technical majors such as environmental sciences, cultural studies or management. [Anna Ehrenberg](/team/anna-ehrenberg/) from AIX supported them in developing their projects and highlighted how much progress they made by building their own models.

## Projects

- **Rooted**: an app that shows the cultural and mythological meaning of a scanned plant, with badges that unlock more information.
- **Demeter**: an app that identifies plants and their diseases from their leaves, for example to use herbicides in a more targeted way.
- **Awareness Fork**: a fork that helps people eat more slowly and mindfully, tested by visitors with a slice of cake.

The projects count towards the students' assessment: the minor uses formats that show AI skills in practice rather than only in written exams. It is offered by the Faculty of Management and Technology as part of the Leuphana AI Campus.
