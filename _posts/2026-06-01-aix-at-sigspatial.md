Our paper, *"Georelating: Inferring Disaster-Affected Areas from Textual Reports,"* introduces a novel task that infers affected geographic areas from natural language reports containing complex locative expressions. This moves beyond traditional geoparsing approaches by reasoning about spatial relations to represent event-affected areas within standardized Discrete Global Grid Systems (DGGSs).

The presentation was well-received, sparking excellent discussions on the future of LLM agents in geospatial reasoning and disaster resilience.
