The AIX Research Group is growing. We are excited to welcome Anna Ehrenberg as our newest Research Associate. 

## Her Focus

Anna will be focusing on Bio-medical Knowledge Graphs and ethical AI alignment. She brings a wealth of experience from her previous work on extracting entity relationships from complex medical text.

She will also be co-teaching the advanced AI lecture focusing on NLP and Knowledge Graphs this upcoming semester.

Welcome to the team, Anna!
