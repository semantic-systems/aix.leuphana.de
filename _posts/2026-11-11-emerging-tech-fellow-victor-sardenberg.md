In October 2026, **Dr. Victor Sardenberg** spent two weeks at the Institute of Information Systems as one of the first four fellows of the **Emerging Tech Fellowship**, hosted by our [Creative Space for Human and Artificial Intelligence](/projects/creative-space/). He is an assistant professor of architectural theory and history at Universidade Presbiteriana Mackenzie in São Paulo, and his research covers computational aesthetics, robotic fabrication and AI.

## The fellowship

The Emerging Tech Lab of Hochschule.digital Niedersachsen started the fellowship in 2026. Its fellows work with universities in Lower Saxony on how AI, extended reality (XR) and other emerging technologies change research, teaching and knowledge transfer.

## Making Worlds

Victor's project, *Making Worlds: AI, XR, and Co-Creative Technologies*, combines a winter school, a symposium and a seminar. He works on it closely with [Martin Kohler](/team/martin-kohler/) and [Ricardo Usbeck](/team/ricardo-usbeck/). During his first stay, he tested ideas for the winter school in the workshop *Making Worlds in Two Hours* at the Creative Space's Salon of Digital Machinations, where participants built and imagined worlds with AI and XR.

Victor returns on 16 January 2027 for the winter school, symposium and seminar, and sees the fellowship as the start of a longer collaboration.
