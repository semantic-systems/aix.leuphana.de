Congratulations to Kai Moltzen and the team! Their paper on "Enhancing Transformer-based Narrative Understanding with Symbolic Logic" has been accepted to the main track of EMNLP 2025.

## About the Paper

Transformer-based Large Language Models (LLMs) often struggle with long-context narrative logic, frequently hallucinating or losing track of state changes. In this paper, we introduce a novel neuro-symbolic architecture that grounds the LLM's output in a symbolic logic engine during the decoding phase.

The results show a significant reduction in hallucination rates on complex narrative reasoning benchmarks.

We look forward to presenting this work in Miami later this year! You can read the pre-print version in our Publications section soon.
