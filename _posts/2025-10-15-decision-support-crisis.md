We are thrilled to announce a new funded collaboration with the City of Hamburg. The AIX group will be developing a neuro-symbolic knowledge graph framework to assist emergency responders with real-time decision support during urban crises.

## Project Overview

In urban crisis scenarios, emergency responders are often overwhelmed with unstructured data from various sources (social media, sensor networks, emergency calls). The goal of this project is to create an explainable, robust AI system that can parse this data, map it to a dynamic Knowledge Graph, and provide actionable, logical deductions.

This project perfectly aligns with our core focus on neuro-symbolic AI and our mission to bring solutions to real-world problems.

Stay tuned for more updates as we begin development next month!
