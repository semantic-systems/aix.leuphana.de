At the start of the academic year in October 2026, Leuphana's Opening Week welcomed 1,500 first-year students under the theme **IN::SECURITY**. One of its four thematic areas, **AI & Autonomy**, was led by [Ricardo Usbeck](/team/ricardo-usbeck/) and supervised by members of AIX.

## Talks and discussions

Ricardo Usbeck gave a talk on how AI challenges established ways of thinking and why society has to decide how to steer the pace of its development. He also interviewed Colonel Sönke Marahrens, an expert on hybrid warfare, about the role of digital media in war and how it is perceived.

## Project groups

AIX members introduced their own research to the student project groups and advised them throughout the week:

- [H. Marlene Schulz](/team/h-marlene-schulz/): *Breaking Bad: AI Agents and the Problem of Autonomy*, on recent security incidents with agent-based AI and the difference between human and machine autonomy.
- [Debayan Banerjee](/team/debayan-banerjee/): *AI and Autonomy: An Opportunity for a Better Future for Germany*, on the risks and opportunities of AI for Germany as a business location and for society.
- [Jannis Gries](/team/jannis-gries/): *Whose Choices? How AI Architectures Shape Human Autonomy*, on decision-making architectures in AI systems and the democratic legitimacy of current AI constitutions.

## Award for "The Alignment Problem"

Each group submitted a critical commentary and a short visionary film. In the AI & Autonomy category, a group supervised by H. Marlene Schulz was awarded for its film *The Alignment Problem* and showed it in the auditorium at the closing event. The film humorously portrays a robot that is not aligned with human intentions, and closes with the point that agent-based AI acts within parameters set by people, so adjusting them is the developers' responsibility.
